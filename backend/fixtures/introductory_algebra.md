## Linear equations

A linear equation in one variable can be written in the form $ax + b = c$ with $a \neq 0$. Solving means isolating $x$ using inverse operations: add or subtract the same value on both sides, then divide by the coefficient of $x$.

Example. Solve $2x + 3 = 11$. Subtract $3$ to get $2x = 8$, then divide by $2$ to get $x = 4$. Check: $2(4)+3=11$.

### Exercises

1. Solve for $x$: $3x - 7 = 14$.
**Answer:** $x = 7$

2. Solve for $x$: $\frac{x}{4} + 5 = 9$.
**Answer:** $x = 16$

## Slope and intercept

The slope-intercept form of a line is $y = mx + b$, where $m$ is the slope and $b$ is the $y$-intercept. Slope between two points $(x_1,y_1)$ and $(x_2,y_2)$ is $m = \frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$ when $x_1 \neq x_2$.

A slope of $0$ is a horizontal line. An undefined slope is a vertical line $x = k$.

### Exercises

3. What is the slope of the line through $(1, 2)$ and $(4, 8)$?
**Answer:** $2$

4. Write the equation of the line with slope $-3$ and $y$-intercept $5$.
**Answer:** $y = -3x + 5$

## Quadratic glimpses

A quadratic $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ with $a \neq 0$ can sometimes be solved by factoring. If $(x-r)(x-s)=0$, then $x=r$ or $x=s$.

### Exercises

5. Solve $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$ by factoring.
**Answer:** $x = 2$ or $x = 3$
